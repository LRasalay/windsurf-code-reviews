Theme: "AI-Augmented Architecture: Empowering Engineers to Build Intelligent, Resilient Systems"
This theme emphasizes how AI is transforming software architecture, enabling faster innovation, smarter decision-making, and more adaptive systems. It positions AI not as a replacement but as a powerful augmentor for architects and engineers, aligning with Capital One's focus on tech-driven financial services. The boot camp will blend foundational architecture principles with practical AI applications, fostering hands-on skills for software engineers and tech leads to design future-proof solutions.
Agenda Overview
The one-day boot camp is structured for an 8-hour format (e.g., 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM), including breaks and interactive elements to keep energy high. Sessions are designed to be progressive: starting with concepts, moving to practical applications, and ending with forward-looking discussions. Allocate time for Q&A in each session, and encourage participation through polls or group activities using tools like Mentimeter or collaborative whiteboards.
Detailed Agenda

9:00 AM - 9:30 AM: Welcome and Kickoff (30 minutes)
Icebreaker: Quick round-robin sharing of participants' current architecture challenges in the AI context (e.g., "How has AI impacted your recent projects?").
Overview of the day's theme and objectives: Highlight how AI is reshaping architecture at Capital One, with examples like AI-enhanced fraud detection or personalized banking experiences.
Speaker: You (as the architect) or a senior leader.
Goal: Set an engaging tone and align everyone on the AI-era mindset.

9:30 AM - 10:30 AM: Session 1 - Foundations of AI in Software Architecture (60 minutes)
Cover core architecture principles (e.g., modularity, scalability, microservices) and how AI introduces new paradigms like data pipelines, model serving, and edge computing.
Discuss AI fundamentals: Machine learning basics, neural networks, and generative AI (e.g., LLMs like Grok or GPT models) tailored for non-AI experts.
Interactive element: Poll on common pain points (e.g., integrating AI without increasing complexity).
Goal: Build a shared vocabulary for engineers and leads to think architecturally about AI.

10:30 AM - 10:45 AM: Coffee Break (15 minutes)
Networking opportunity; provide AI-themed prompts for casual discussions (e.g., "What's one AI tool you're excited to try?").

10:45 AM - 12:00 PM: Session 2 - Designing AI-Integrated Architectures (75 minutes)
Dive into patterns: Event-driven architectures with AI (e.g., Kafka for real-time data), API designs for AI services, and hybrid cloud setups (e.g., AWS SageMaker integration).
Address challenges: Data privacy (GDPR/CCPA compliance at Capital One), bias mitigation, and ethical AI design.
Case study: A simplified Capital One-inspired example, like architecting an AI-powered recommendation engine for credit card offers.
Goal: Equip attendees with blueprints for incorporating AI into existing systems.

12:00 PM - 1:00 PM: Lunch Break (60 minutes)
Informal group discussions or AI demo stations (e.g., quick trials of tools like GitHub Copilot for code architecture).

1:00 PM - 2:30 PM: Session 3 - Hands-On Workshop: AI Tools for Architectural Excellence (90 minutes)
Practical exercises: Use tools like Draw.io with AI plugins, Lucidchart AI, or code-based tools (e.g., Python with NetworkX for system modeling).
Activities: In small groups, redesign a sample microservice architecture to include AI components (e.g., adding a predictive analytics layer).
Provide templates and guidance for tech leads to facilitate.
Goal: Hands-on application to reinforce learning and spark creativity.

2:30 PM - 2:45 PM: Afternoon Break (15 minutes)
Quick stretch or AI trivia to re-energize.

2:45 PM - 3:45 PM: Session 4 - Scaling and Securing AI Architectures (60 minutes)
Topics: Performance optimization (e.g., GPU acceleration, containerization with Kubernetes), security best practices (e.g., AI model hardening against adversarial attacks), and monitoring (e.g., using Prometheus with AI metrics).
Real-world insights: Lessons from industry failures/successes, like AI scaling in fintech.
Interactive: Group brainstorming on risk assessment for AI deployments.
Goal: Prepare leads for production-ready AI architectures.

3:45 PM - 4:45 PM: Session 5 - Future Trends and Panel Discussion (60 minutes)
Explore emerging trends: AI agents, multimodal models, and quantum-inspired computing's impact on architecture.
Panel: Invite 2-3 guest speakers (e.g., Capital One AI experts or external thought leaders) for a moderated Q&A.
Attendees submit questions in advance or live.
Goal: Inspire long-term thinking and networking.

4:45 PM - 5:00 PM: Wrap-Up and Next Steps (15 minutes)
Key takeaways recap and feedback survey.
Action items: Share resources (e.g., GitHub repos, reading lists on AI architecture books like "Designing Data-Intensive Applications").
Closing: Motivational note on how attendees can drive AI innovation at Capital One.
Goal: End on a high note with actionable insights.
